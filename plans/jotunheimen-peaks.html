Where we are

Rides all the way to Fondsbu — the first 27.8 km of the route never got walked. We are at the hut on Saturday, exactly where the old plan put camp 2, but with two days of legs still in the bank.

The two peaks on the list

Slettmarkpiggen 2164 → swap for Galdebergtinden 2075

Slettmarkpiggen sits 0.7 km north of our own trail high point — but 570 m straight up a steep face. The usual line goes over Slettmarkkampen (2032) with a ~25 m grade III step and exposed rock after it, with Slettmarkbrean underneath. That is roped climbing, not hiking — two of us, no rope, no helmet, no phone signal, Torfinnsbu locked, 21 km from a road, late September. Not this trip.

Galdebergtinden 2075 leaves from the same saddle: a ledge system west onto the ridge, then easy ground to the top. No climbing, no glacier. Same 2000-metre summit, and the best seat in the house for looking at Slettmarkpiggen.

From the saddle 1.6 km each wayUp +480 mTime 2½–3½ h

Langeskavltinden 2014 → leave it

It is a walking peak (cairns, no trail on the ridge) but it goes north-west from Fondsbu: ~11 km each way, +950 m, 8–10 h. Two problems:

  • It is off the edge of our printed sheets — sheet N2 stops at 459 E and the approach past Sløtatjernet runs straight off the paper.
  • Spending a day on it leaves 33.8 km for Monday + Tuesday morning: two 17 km days, the second one finishing against the last bus. One bad-weather day and the whole chain home breaks.

Save it for a trip that starts and ends at Tyin.

Before we walk out of Fondsbu

  • Ask about DNT membership + a standard key. It turns Torfinnsbu at km 21.3 from a locked box into a roof — worth a lot now that we are going higher and later.
  • Food for four days. Nothing between here and Bygdisheim.
  • Charge everything. Ask the staff for the yr.no print-out.
  • Tell the desk our route and our bus.

Turn-around rules

  • Galdebergtinden: if the rock is iced or the cloud is on the ridge, walk past it. The saddle is on the way home either way.
  • Light: sunset 19:35, properly dark 20:15 — and four minutes earlier each day.
  • Last exit backwards is Fondsbu until km 11.5. After the saddle it is always shorter to carry on.
  • 112 emergency · 113 medical · DNT Oslo +47 22 82 28 00
